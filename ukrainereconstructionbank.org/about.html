Our Mission

The Ukraine Reconstruction Bank (URB) coordinates the $800 billion international effort for Ukraine's recovery. Operating under the EU-US "Prosperity Plan," we transition emergency assistance into long-term private investment.

We are the credibility anchor for Western capital, positioning the United States not just as a donor, but as a strategic economic partner and investor alongside the European Commission.

Our mandate is defined by legislation to ensure that every dollar, euro, and hryvnia is accounted for and deployed with maximum efficiency.
