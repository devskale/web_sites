Our Mission

The Ukraine Reconstruction Bank (URB) was established to serve as the primary financial coordination hub for the rebuilding of Ukraine. We act as the bridge between international donor funds, private capital, and the critical infrastructure projects on the ground.

Our mandate is defined by legislation to ensure that every dollar, euro, and hryvnia is accounted for and deployed with maximum efficiency.
