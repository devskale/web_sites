# Ukraine Reconstruction Bank (URB) - Website Structure Plan

This document outlines the proposed structure for the Ukraine Reconstruction Bank (URB) website. The site is designed to serve as a hub for international investment, project funding, and transparent reporting on the reconstruction efforts in Ukraine.

## 1. Core Principles
*   **Transparency:** Real-time dashboards of funded projects and fund utilization.
*   **Accessibility:** Clear pathways for both Investors (contributors) and Applicants (project owners).
*   **Security:** High-level compliance and vetting standards (KYC/AML).
*   **Bilingual:** Full support for English (EN) and Ukrainian (UA).

## 2. Sitemap

### 2.1. Home Page (`/`)
*   **Hero Section:** High-quality visuals of modern Ukraine/reconstruction projects. Tagline: "Invest in Tomorrow's Ukraine."
*   **Quick Stats (Live Counter):**
    *   Capital Mobilized ($)
    *   Projects Funded (#)
    *   Jobs Created (#)
*   **Mission Statement:** Brief overview of the URB's role in coordinating international aid and private equity.
*   **Featured Sectors:** Grid of icons/cards linking to Investment Focus areas (Energy, Infrastructure, etc.).
*   **Latest News:** 3 most recent press releases or success stories.
*   **CTA Buttons:** "Apply for Funding" | "Become an Investor"

### 2.2. About Us (`/about`)
*   **Our Story:** History of establishment and partnership with global entities (e.g., DFC, EBRD).
*   **Leadership Team:** Profiles of the Board of Directors and Executive Management.
*   **Governance & Ethics:** Anti-corruption policies, auditing standards, and oversight committees.
*   **Partners:** Logos of strategic partners (Governments, International Banks, NGOs).
*   **Location:**
    *   **Headquarters:** 77 Sichovih Striltsiv Street, 6th Floor, Kyiv, 04053, Ukraine.

### 2.3. Investment Focus (`/sectors`)
*   **Infrastructure:** Roads, bridges, schools, and hospitals.
*   **Energy Security:** Renewable energy projects, grid modernization, nuclear safety.
*   **Housing & Urban Planning:** Residential reconstruction, smart city initiatives.
*   **Agriculture:** Food security, machinery, and logistics/export support.
*   **Technology & Innovation:** Digital infrastructure and tech startups.
*   **Critical Minerals:** Extraction and processing projects.

### 2.4. For Project Applicants (`/apply`)
*   **Eligibility Criteria:** Checklist for businesses and municipalities.
*   **Funding Products:**
    *   Direct Loans
    *   Equity Investment
    *   Political Risk Insurance
    *   Technical Assistance Grants
*   **Application Process:** Step-by-step guide (Submission -> Due Diligence -> Approval -> Disbursement).
*   **Secure Portal Login:** Link to `portal.urb.ua` for submissions.

### 2.5. For Investors (`/investors`)
*   **Investment Instruments:** Bonds, Green Funds, Direct Equity.
*   **Risk Management:** Compliance frameworks and strict auditing.
*   **Impact Reporting:** Detailed ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) reports.
*   **Investor Relations:** Contact for institutional investors.

### 2.6. News & Media (`/media`)
*   **Press Releases:** Official announcements.
*   **Reports:** Monthly/Quarterly financial and progress reports (PDF downloads).
*   **Gallery:** Photo and video documentation of reconstruction progress.

### 2.7. Contact (`/contact`)
*   **General Inquiries:** Contact form.
*   **Whistleblower Hotline:** Anonymous reporting channel for integrity violations.
*   **Office Address:**
    *   Ukraine Reconstruction Bank
    *   77 Sichovih Striltsiv Street, 6th Floor
    *   Kyiv, 04053
    *   Ukraine
*   **Map:** Google Maps embedding of the Kyiv location.

## 3. Technical Requirements
*   **CMS:** Headless CMS for easy content management in multiple languages.
*   **Security:** SSL/TLS encryption, DDoS protection, GDPR compliance.
*   **Performance:** Fast loading times optimized for low-bandwidth environments.

## 4. Visual Identity
*   **Colors:** Blue and Yellow (Ukraine National Colors) accented with professional Dark Grey and White.
*   **Typography:** Clean, modern san-serif fonts (e.g., Inter, Roboto).
*   **Imagery:** Authentic photography of construction, technology, and people; avoid generic stock photos.
